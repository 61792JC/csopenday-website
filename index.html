About Me

Janice is effervescent, warm and a lot of fun. A down-to-earth Hamiltonian she has come full circle, via London and Sydney to lay down her own family roots here in the Waikato. Back in her home-town she thinks Hamilton is the perfect place to bring up a young family; safe but connected, accessible yet evolving.

Janice was a seamstress for one season of the Royal NZ Ballet Company’s production of Romeo & Juliet and subsequently was accepted into the London College of Fashion Costume Design degree course. Financial circumstances thwarted the dream and she became a Business Class flight attendant with Qantas which brought her all over the world.

Harbouring a burning desire to get back into the rag trade, she filled her creative tank by enrolling on numerous short courses at the London College of Fashion on her down time from travelling. Millinery, styling, fashion buying and merchandising she has clocked up on her resume and on her credit card bill stunning pieces from Rome, Paris, New York, Melbourne and LA!

Janice can entertain you with amusing anecdotes from her experience hosting in First Class. She has attended to the rich and famous, rock and sporting royalty. She is the consummate hostess – personable, discreet and responsive. Stevie Wonder found her so amusing he gave her a shout out at a couple of his Australian concerts.

Wardrobe Edit - Custom outfit curation.

To curate a workable and versatile wardrobe we have to do an audit. Between us we can decide what stays, what goes and what’s a maybe, build an update/alterations pile and then decide what to donate to your charity of choice or deserving friend.

We will identify gaps, put new outfit suggestions together and create a shopping list with our recommendations.

You will be left with a clutter free wardrobe with clothes that you truly love. You will never again utter the words “I have nothing to wear” but feel excited about future shopping trips.

Body Type Analysis - Size doesn't matter but shape does.

Understanding how to dress for your body shape is the fundamental principle of style. Learn how to dress your proportions to best advantage. We’ll tell you what looks good on you and take the guesswork out of shopping, no more mistakes just smart clothing choices.

Together we’ll work out your clothing personality by looking at shop look books and browsing through fashion magazines. We’ll find out a little bit more about your lifestyle and style aspirations and leave you with a positive attitude towards your image.

You will also receive an inventory of outfit ideas and store recommendations tailored to your shape and lifestyle. Expect to feel inspired.
