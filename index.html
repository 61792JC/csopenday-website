About Me

Janice is effervescent, warm and a lot of fun. A down-to-earth Hamiltonian she has come full circle, via London and Sydney to lay down her own family roots here in the Waikato. Back in her home-town she thinks Hamilton is the perfect place to bring up a young family; safe but connected, accessible yet evolving. Janice was a seamstress for one season of the Royal NZ Ballet Company’s production of Romeo & Juliet and subsequently was accepted into the London College of Fashion Costume Design degree course. Financial circumstances thwarted the dream and she became a Business Class flight attendant with Qantas which brought her all over the world. Harbouring a burning desire to get back into the rag trade, she filled her creative tank by enrolling on numerous short courses at the London College of Fashion on her down time from travelling. Millinery, styling, fashion buying and merchandising she has clocked up on her resume and on her credit card bill stunning pieces from Rome, Paris, New York, Melbourne and LA! Janice can entertain you with amusing anecdotes from her experience hosting in First Class. She has attended to the rich and famous, rock and sporting royalty. She is the consummate hostess – personable, discreet and responsive. Stevie Wonder found her so amusing he gave her a shout out at a couple of his Australian concerts.
